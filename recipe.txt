Soak rice 20 minutes. Parboil with salt and whole spices until ~70% cooked; drain.

Fry onions in oil/ghee till golden; keep some aside for garnish.

Cook chicken: Add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, turmeric, biryani masala, salt, yogurt. Cook chicken till ~80% done.

Layer: In the same pot, spread chicken, then rice. Top with fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk.

Dum (steam): Cover tightly and cook on low heat 10-15 minutes.

Serve hot.
