Ingredients
- 2 cups basmati rice (soaked 30 min)
- 500 g chicken (bone-in preferred)
- 1 cup yogurt
- 2 large onions (thinly sliced, fried until golden)
- 2 tomatoes (chopped)
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 3–4 green chilies (slit)
- ½ cup fresh coriander leaves
- ½ cup mint leaves
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp garam masala powder
- 1 tsp biryani masala (optional)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 4 tbsp ghee or oil
- Whole spices: 4 cloves, 2 cardamoms, 1 bay leaf, 1 cinnamon stick
- ½ cup milk with a few saffron strands soaked
Method
- Marinate chicken: Mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, lemon juice, and salt. Rest for 1–2 hours.
- Cook rice: Boil water with whole spices and salt. Add soaked rice, cook until 70% done, then drain.
- Prepare chicken masala: Heat ghee/oil, sauté onions until golden, add tomatoes, mint, coriander, and marinated chicken. Cook until chicken is 80% done.
- Layering: In a heavy-bottomed pot, spread half the chicken masala, then half the rice. Sprinkle fried onions, saffron milk, mint, and coriander. Repeat with remaining chicken and rice.
- Dum cooking: Seal the pot with dough or a tight lid. Cook on low flame for 25–30 minutes.
- Serve: Fluff gently and serve hot with raita and salan.
