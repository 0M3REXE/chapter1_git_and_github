Marinate

Mix chicken with all marinade ingredients. Cover and rest at least 1 hour (overnight in fridge is best).
Prepare rice

Wash and soak basmati rice for 20-30 minutes.
Boil water with whole spices and salt.
Cook rice until 70% done (grains should still have a slight bite). Drain and set aside.
Cook the chicken masala

Heat oil/ghee in a heavy pot.
Fry sliced onions until golden brown.
Add green chillies and tomatoes. Cook till soft.
Add marinated chicken and cook covered on medium until chicken is 80% done and oil separates.
Layering

Spread half the rice over chicken.

Sprinkle mint, coriander, saffron milk, and a little ghee.

Add remaining rice and repeat garnish.
Dum (Steam cooking)

Cover tightly (use foil or dough to seal).
Cook on very low heat for 20-25 minutes.
Let it rest 10 minutes before opening.
Serve With

Raita (curd + onion + salt + cumin)

Salan or simple sliced onions with lemon
