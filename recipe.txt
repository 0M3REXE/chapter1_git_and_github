Method
- Cook rice: Boil rice with cardamom, cloves, and salt until 70–75% done. Drain and set aside.
- Fry onions & nuts: In ghee, caramelize onions until golden. Remove. Fry cashews until golden.
- Pan-fry veggies: Lightly sauté potato, cauliflower, beans, and carrot for texture.
- Prepare saffron milk: Warm milk, add saffron strands, and let sit.
- Make masala: Heat oil, add cumin, cinnamon, bay leaf, peppercorns, cardamom, cloves. Sauté onions, then ginger-garlic-chili paste. Mix in yogurt, veggies, biryani masala, chili powder, and cook until thick.
- Layering: Grease a heavy-bottom pan with ghee. Add half rice, fried onion, cashews, herbs, rose water. Spread veggie masala. Top with remaining rice, onion, cashews, herbs, saffron milk, rose water, and drizzle ghee.
- Dum cooking: Seal with f- oil and lid. Place pan on a hot tawa, cook on lowest heat for 25–30 minutes.
- Serve: Scoop from the bottom so each portion has rice + veggies. Pair with raita or salan.
