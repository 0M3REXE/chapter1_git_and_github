👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking
Step 1: Prepare the Rice
- Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes.
- Boil water with salt and a few whole spices (bay leaf, cloves, cardamom).
- Cook rice until 70% done (grains should be firm). Drain and set aside.
Step 2: Cook the Chicken
- Heat oil + ghee in a large pan.
- Fry sliced onions until golden brown (reserve half for garnish).
- Add ginger-garlic paste, sauté until fragrant.
- Add chicken pieces, turmeric, chili powder, and salt. Cook until chicken is sealed.
- Add chopped tomatoes, yogurt, mint, and coriander. Cook until chicken is tender and gravy thickens.
Step 3: Layering the Biryani
- In a heavy-bottom pot, spread half the 
- chicken mixture.
- Add a layer of rice on top.
- Sprinkle fried onions, saffron milk, and a pinch of garam masala.
- Repeat layers (chicken → rice → garnish).
Step 4: Dum Cooking (Sealing & Steaming)
- Cover tightly with a lid (or seal with dough for authentic style).
- Cook on low flame for 20–25 minutes.
- Let it rest for 10 minutes before opening.

🍽️ Serving
- Gently mix layers before serving.
- Pair with raita (yogurt + cucumber/onion) and salad.
