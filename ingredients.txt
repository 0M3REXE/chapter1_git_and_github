🥘 Ingredients
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 500 g chicken (cut into medium pieces)
- 2 large onions (thinly sliced)
- 2 tomatoes (chopped)
- 1 cup yogurt
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2–3 green chilies (slit)
- Fresh coriander & mint leaves (a handful each)
- Whole spices: 2 bay leaves, 4 cloves, 2 cardamoms, 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp biryani masala (optional)
- 4 tbsp oil + 2 tbsp ghee
- Saffron strands soaked in ¼ cup warm milk (or food color as substitute)
- Salt to taste
